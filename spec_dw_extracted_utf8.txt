1. GENERAL PRESENTATION
1.1 Document Content
This document constitutes the Detailed Functional Specification (DFS). It describes all functional requirements, decision-making indicators, analysis axes, decision-makers, as well as the technical and decision-making architecture of the BI system, in alignment with the selected constellation model.
1.2 Context and Objectives
Each season, the scout group manages a large volume of data related to members, scout units, activities, camps, and budgets. These data are currently used in an administrative manner.
The objective of this project is to transform these data into decision-oriented information in order to improve governance, strategic planning, and decision-making.

5. PREDICTIVE ANALYTICS AND ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
This section presents the Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence objectives defined by the client and integrated into the BI solution in order to enhance decision support.
5.1 Objectives of Predictive Analytics (Machine Learning)
The objective of the predictive analytics component is to exploit historical data to anticipate future trends and support proactive decision-making.
The main Machine Learning objectives are:
Membership Forecasting and Organizational Planning
The system will predict the future number of members per scout unit and per period in order to anticipate organizational needs, including staffing requirements, activity planning, and logistical preparation. This forecasting capability will support proactive management and long-term planning.
Budget Estimation and Financial Planning
Based on historical activity volumes, participation levels, and cost patterns, the system will estimate the required budgets for upcoming activities and camps. This objective aims to improve financial planning accuracy and optimize resource allocation.
Participation Rate Prediction
Participation rates in activities and camps will be predicted using historical attendance data and participation trends. These predictions will enable better planning, improve engagement strategies, and reduce uncertainty in activity organization.
Unit Performance Classification
Scout units will be classified according to their overall performance level (low, medium, or high) based on key indicators such as participation, growth, activity frequency, and retention. This classification will facilitate comparative analysis and targeted decision-making.
Identification of At-Risk Units
The system will identify scout units at risk of membership decline or disengagement by analyzing trends in participation, retention rates, and engagement indicators. This objective supports early intervention and preventive management actions.
Behavioral Segmentation of Scout Units
Scout units will be segmented according to their behavioral patterns and organizational dynamics. This segmentation will help decision-makers understand different unit profiles and adapt management strategies accordingly.
Weather-Aware Predictive Modeling
A weather-aware predictive model will be developed by combining historical weather data, geographical localization information, and activity and camp KPIs. The model will anticipate adverse climatic conditions such as storms, extreme temperatures, and heavy rainfall, and evaluate their potential impact on planned activities and camps.
Intelligent Activity Adaptation and Rescheduling
Based on weather forecasts and historical performance data, an intelligent activity adaptation model will recommend alternative activities, activity rescheduling, or relocation when unfavorable weather conditions are predicted for a specific location. This approach aims to minimize operational disruptions and ensure activity continuity.
Early Warning and Proactive Alert System
An early warning system will continuously monitor KPIs and predictive indicators to proactively notify decision-makers and unit leaders of potential operational risks related to weather, location, or participation levels. Alerts will be generated before critical thresholds are reached, enabling preventive rather than reactive actions.
Financial Anomaly and Fraud Detection
A dedicated anomaly detection model will be implemented to identify abnormal patterns in budget consumption, participation rates, or camp costs. These anomalies may indicate financial irregularities, data inconsistencies, or inefficient resource usage, thereby supporting financial transparency and risk mitigation.
Predictive Member Engagement Scoring
Member-level analytics will be enhanced through the introduction of a predictive engagement score calculated based on participation history, attendance patterns, and activity involvement. This score will enable early identification of disengagement risks and support targeted intervention strategies by unit leaders.
5.2 Advanced Artificial Intelligence Objectives
Beyond predictive analysis, the project aims to integrate advanced Artificial Intelligence mechanisms to reinforce strategic decision-making.
The main AI objectives are:
Decision Support System
The system will provide a simple decision support mechanism that presents analytical results, predictive indicators, and KPIs in a clear and structured way. This system will help decision-makers understand the situation and choose appropriate actions without relying on complex AI models.
Automatic Recommendation Generation
Basic automatic recommendations will be generated based on predefined rules and KPI thresholds. For example, the system may suggest priority actions or resource adjustments when certain indicators exceed or fall below defined limits.
Weak Signal Detection
The system will monitor gradual changes in KPIs to identify early warning signs of potential organizational or financial risks. This detection will rely on trend analysis and simple threshold-based logic rather than complex AI algorithms.
Scenario Analysis Support
The system will allow decision-makers to explore simple ôwhat-ifö scenarios by modifying key parameters such as budget levels, number of activities, or expected participation. The impact of these changes will be evaluated using historical averages and predictive outputs.
Recommendation and Alert System
A lightweight recommendation and alert system will be implemented to notify decision-makers when predefined risk conditions are detected. Alerts and suggestions will be generated based on predictive results, weather conditions, and location data, using clear and understandable rules.
Conversational Chatbot 
A basic conversational chatbot will be developed to answer frequently asked questions related to activities, camps, participation, and alerts. The chatbot will rely on predefined responses and simple personalization based on user roles rather than advanced natural language understanding.
Proactive Risk Monitoring
The system will continuously track key KPIs and predictive indicators to identify potential risks related to participation, budget, or environmental conditions. When abnormal patterns are detected, the system will trigger alerts to support early intervention.

These users represent the primary decision-makers within the Scout Group and are directly involved in managing units, activities, and camps.
Users:
Scout Leaders

Monitor the evolution of members within their unit

Track the number of activities organized per period
Evaluate camp organization and participation levels
Identify potential issues related to engagement or retention

Retention rate
